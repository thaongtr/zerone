INSIGHT

Nearly 80%

of study participants said they would not return after a healthcare experience that undermined their trust.

Clinical expertise is essential. Yet patients experience care through every interaction — from finding the right professional to receiving support after a visit.

Understanding where these experiences break down is where meaningful healthcare innovation begins.

Source: Deloitte Insights, 2021. Focus groups with 525 US consumers (Black, Asian, Hispanic, Native American); not nationally representative.

A founder’s perspective

Healthcare innovation starts with understanding how people experience care.

Working with healthcare professionals and practices has shown us that many challenges extend beyond clinical expertise — into how patients discover services, communicate, make decisions and continue care.

At Zerone, we bring together these real-world insights, applied research, experience design and AI to develop more intuitive ways for healthcare professionals and patients to stay connected.

Our ambition is not simply to digitize existing processes, but to rethink how care can work better for everyone involved.
