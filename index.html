A founder’s perspective

Most doctors and healthcare founders I meet are not trying to “build a brand.”

They want the right patients to understand the value of their expertise, trust their approach and feel confident choosing them.

But patients cannot fully assess clinical quality before receiving care. They form trust through what they can see, understand and experience, from how a clinic communicates to how care is delivered.

That is why I believe healthcare branding should do more than create recognition. It should make the value of care visible and carry it consistently through the patient experience.

This belief led to Trust Translation™, Zerone’s framework for turning healthcare value into patient trust.

Nguyen Tran Linh ThaoFounder & strategic lead

Registered pharmacist with nine years of experience designing patient experience in clinics and developing training programmes for healthcare teams.
