Privacy Policy

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account. If you book a call, you share your details through a Google Form: typically your name, role, contact details and a short note about what you want to build. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to you, arrange a call and understand how Zerone can help. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Website usage counts

To understand which pages are useful, we count page views and clicks on buttons, without cookies and without identifying you. Each count records only the page, its language, the type of source you came from (for example search or social), the name of the referring site (for example www.linkedin.com, never the full address), a campaign code if the link had one, and how many seconds the page was open. The counts we keep contain no IP address, no visitor ID and no personal data, and are stored on Supabase servers. We respect your browser’s Do Not Track setting.

Who processes it

Form responses are stored by Google. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so those providers may receive technical data such as your IP address when you visit.

Please do not send patient information

This website is for business enquiries. Do not send patient or health information through the form, by email or in any other way.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you have shared at any time by emailing contact@zerone.vn.
