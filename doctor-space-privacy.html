Doctor Space Privacy Policy

Version: privacy-ds-2026-11-09-v1 · Effective: 09/11/2026 · The Vietnamese version prevails.

This policy describes the personal information of Doctor Space subscribers that Zerone processes when you place an order on zerone.vn and when Zerone activates and runs your plan. It states only what the system actually processes. Patient data in the Zerone Mini App is covered by the Mini App privacy policy, not this one, and that feature is not switched on when you subscribe.

1. Who processes it

ZERONE COMPANY LIMITED (CÔNG TY TNHH ZERONE, enterprise code 0318035131), L18-11-13, Floor 18, Vincom Center Dong Khoi, 72 Le Thanh Ton, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City. Privacy contact: contact@zerone.vn · 0901 456 711. Contact person: Nguyen Tran Linh Thao, Founder. Zerone decides the purposes of the processing below.

2. Information we collect

When you place an order:

  • Your field of practice (medicine, mental health, veterinary), full name, and practice name if you enter one.
  • The way you chose to be contacted (email, phone or Zalo) and the matching contact details.
  • The plan, the amount and the payment reference of the order, and when it was placed.
  • Proof of acceptance: the version of the Terms and the Privacy Policy you agreed to, when you agreed, the display language, and your confirmation that you subscribe for professional purposes.
  • A one-way hash of your network address, only to prevent abuse. We do not store the raw IP address for this purpose.

When you pay:

  • The bank transaction ID, the amount, the time, and the result of matching it to your order. We do not store the payer's name, the payer's account number or any card details.

When Zerone runs your plan: the professional profile, services, prices and credentials you provide or confirm, the status and term of your plan, and an administrative log of who changed what and when.

We do not collect payment card details, your contacts or your location. If card payment is activated through the AppotaPay gateway, the card details are processed by the gateway; Zerone receives only the transaction result and a payment token if you actively sign up for automatic renewal.

3. Why we process it

  • To create and match orders, confirm receipt of money, and activate and manage the plan (performing the contract you ask for).
  • To contact you about the order and activation (your consent). We send a confirmation email after receiving your payment if you left an email address.
  • To keep proof of acceptance, invoices, receipts and the administrative log (legal obligation and legitimate interest).
  • To keep the system safe and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with others to market to you.

4. Who handles or receives it

  • Authorised Zerone staff, to process orders, activate plans and give support; access is controlled by permission and logged.
  • Supabase (database and server functions) stores the information on servers in Singapore.
  • Resend sends your confirmation email; it contains your name, plan, amount, payment reference and term. Resend's servers are in Japan.
  • SePay (a bank-transaction notification service) receives the details of transfers into Zerone's account: amount, transfer content, time and what the bank provides, in order to notify Zerone.
  • AppotaPay (online payment gateway, when activated) receives the information needed to process a transaction you make and returns the result to Zerone.
  • VietQR (img.vietqr.io) draws the QR image from Zerone's account number, the amount and the payment reference; it does not receive your personal information.
  • Cloudflare, GitHub Pages and Google Fonts run the website and the admin application and may receive technical data such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Competent state authorities, when the law requires.

Part of the information is stored or processed outside Vietnam (Singapore, Japan). Zerone prepares the dossiers and safeguards required by Vietnamese law on transferring personal data abroad.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order information and proof of acceptance while we provide the service and for as long as the law requires accounting, tax and electronic-contract records to be kept. After that we delete or anonymise it, unless the law requires longer retention.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing, withdraw consent, complain and claim compensation as the law provides. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Send your request to contact@zerone.vn with your order's payment reference; we reply within the time the law sets. Some information that must be kept by law (for example payment records) cannot be deleted during that period.

7. Protecting data

We use encryption in transit, role-based permissions, access to order tables closed to anonymous users, an administrative log, and we allow payment confirmation only through the server key or an authorised administrator. No system is perfectly safe; if an incident affects your information we will tell you and the competent authority as required.

8. Your browser

While you place an order your browser keeps your plan choice and what you typed in the tab's session storage so you do not have to type it again; it is cleared when you close the tab and is not sent anywhere until you press create order. The order page uses no advertising cookies.

9. Changes

When the policy changes we publish a new version with its number and date. If it changes materially we will ask for your consent again at your next order.
