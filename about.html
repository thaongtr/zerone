Nguyen Tran Linh Thao

Nguyễn Trần Linh Thảo

Nguyen Tran Linh Thao is a Vietnamese pharmacist and founder of Zerone, working at the intersection of patient experience, healthcare innovation, and healthcare service design. Her work focuses on connecting healthcare expertise with how people discover, access, and experience care.

At Zerone, she develops Trust Translation, an approach that helps doctors' expertise become something patients can recognize, understand and trust.

A pharmacist who grew up packing doctors' orders in her mother's pharmacy, Linh Thao believes that patient trust does not begin in the consulting room; it begins with how people look for care, make contact and are welcomed. She has helped build and deliver online professional education programmes for doctors.

In 2023, she founded Zerone, a healthcare brand and patient experience consultancy that is also building a network to help patients find and book doctors. She cares about the future of the doctor–patient relationship in Vietnam, where clarity and respect are the foundation of trust.
