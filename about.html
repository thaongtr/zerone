Pharmacist · Founder of Zerone

Nguyen Tran Linh Thao

From the age of six, Linh Thao helped pack and arrange medicine for doctors at the family pharmacy — the starting point of a lifelong bond with healthcare.

With experience in healthcare project development, professional education and applied research on behaviour and patient experience, Linh Thao brings Zerone a perspective that combines strategy, expertise and the care experience.

Selected experience

  • 1M+Contributed to market access strategy and model development for an immunization program targeting over 1 million people.
  • 50,000+Joined the team that developed Vietnam’s first online CME/CPE platform, connecting a network of over 50,000 doctors and pharmacists nationwide.
  • NationwidePartnered with leading pharmaceutical companies on healthcare-professional engagement strategy and on collaboration and medical education programs at national scale.
  • Zerone SpacePioneering an innovative model connecting doctors and patients on Zalo, a platform with nearly 80 million users, helping doctors strengthen their professional presence and users easily find, choose and stay engaged with the right professionals.
